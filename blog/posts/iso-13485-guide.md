# Understanding ISO 13485 in Medical Sourcing

**Author:** Wells Wan, Head of Quality  
**Date:** January 2025

For medical device distributors, **ISO 13485** is not just a certificate; it is a language of trust.

## Why it Matters?
Unlike ISO 9001 which focuses on "customer satisfaction", ISO 13485 focuses on **safety and efficacy**.

> "In the medical field, a small defect is not a refund issue; it's a patient safety issue."

## V5 Medical's Approach
We only work with manufacturing partners who maintain valid ISO 13485 certification from reputable notified bodies (TUV, SGS, etc.)...
