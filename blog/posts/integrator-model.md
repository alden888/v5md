# Why the "Integrator Model" is the Future of Medical Procurement

**Date:** December 25, 2025  
**Author:** Alden Cheng, Managing Director

In the traditional trade model, buyers had to choose between:
1. **Factories:** Low price, but limited product range and high MOQ.
2. **Trading Companies:** High service, but high markup and opaque quality control.

## The V5 Solution
