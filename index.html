Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our medical products and services.

What certifications do your products have?

Our products are manufactured in strict compliance with international standards. We hold ISO 13485, CE, and FDA certifications, ensuring that our medical devices are safe and effective for use in global markets, including Europe and the USA.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

We strive to be flexible. For most standard items, our MOQ starts from just 1 carton. However, for custom-branded or specialized surgical packs, MOQs may vary. Please contact our sales team for specific details.

Do you provide product samples?

Yes, we provide samples for quality evaluation. Sample costs and shipping fees apply initially, but these costs can be deducted from your first bulk order. We believe in the quality of our products and want you to test them with confidence.
