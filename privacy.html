2.2 Information Collected Automatically

4. Data Security

We implement strict technical, administrative, and physical security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Our payment processing partners utilize industry-standard encryption protocols. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
