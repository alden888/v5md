Our Story

20 Years of Medical Innovation

Founded in 2005, V5 Medical started with a simple vision: to bridge the gap between high-quality medical manufacturing and global healthcare needs.

Today, we have grown into a comprehensive supplier with strategic manufacturing bases in Shandong, Suqian, and Shenzhen. We specialize in surgical sutures, sterile packs, and injection systems, serving hospitals and distributors in over 40 countries.
