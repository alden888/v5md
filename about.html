Our History

20 Years of Medical Innovation

Founded in 2005, V5 Medical started with a vision to bridge the gap between high-quality medical manufacturing and global healthcare needs. What began as a small suture production line has grown into a comprehensive medical supply powerhouse.

Today, we operate strategic manufacturing bases in Shandong, Suqian, and Shenzhen, specializing in surgical sutures, sterile packs, and injection systems for hospitals in over 40 countries.
