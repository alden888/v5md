OUR HISTORY

20 Years of Medical Innovation

Founded in 2005, VS Medical started with a vision to bridge the gap between high-quality medical manufacturing and global healthcare needs. What began as a small future production line has grown into a comprehensive medical supply powerhouse.

Today, we serve healthcare providers in over 50 countries with a portfolio of more than 500 medical products. Our commitment to innovation, quality control, and affordable pricing has established us as a trusted partner in the global healthcare community.
